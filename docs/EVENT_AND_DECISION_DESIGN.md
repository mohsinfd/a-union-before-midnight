# Event and Decision Design

## Player-facing rule

A **decision** is an optional authorization the player may defer until the
political, military or economic moment is right. Its cost and prerequisites
must be visible before it can be selected.

An **event** is something the government cannot quietly postpone: the transfer
of power, a constitutional deadline, an external crisis, a foreign reply, an
implementation dispute, or a milestone caused by an earlier decision.

Automatic one-option events may deliver consequences or rewards, but they may
not silently deduct money, supplies or manpower. Costly single-path programmes
must be decisions.

## Historical premise

The opening divergence grows from real constitutional pressure points: the
1930-1932 Round Table Conferences, the 1932 Poona settlement, the proposed
all-India federation, and the British White Paper process that began in 1933.
The early transfer of sovereignty, its exact coalition and its terms are
deliberate alternate history.
