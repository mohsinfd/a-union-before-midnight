# A Union Before Midnight 4.2 Alpha 14

## Tokyo Recovery

- Repairs the slept Tokyo assessment dispatcher that stranded second partnership
  proposals in older saves.
- Adds a one-time, no-cost legacy-save recovery for pending proposals after the
  Tokyo channel has reopened.
- Credits the China-policy concession, recognition of Japan's China position
  and the Indian-led Imphal plan in the influence ledger.
- Keeps the normal Japanese response roll: senior India receives 70 percent
  acceptance, 25 percent counteroffer and 5 percent rejection.
- Makes the formal Delhi-Tokyo alliance decision available through the War
  Cabinet after a strategic partnership is established.

## Diplomatic Clarity

- Shows costs and response odds before the major London, Berlin, Moscow, Tokyo,
  Delhi Pact, Himalayan, Ceylon, Goa and Abyssinian choices.
- Preserves curated consequence text through future build-and-deploy runs.
- Gives the expensive dual Himalayan offer real leverage: Bhutan moves from
  60 to 75 percent acceptance and Nepal from 45 to 60 percent.
- Makes Goa escalation methods materially different: immediate ultimatum 35
  percent, customs pressure 55 percent, civil resistance 65 percent and a
  sustained blockade 75 percent.
- Adds response ledgers to the Allied, German, Soviet and non-aligned strategic
  conferences.

## Naval Contracts

- Corrects Project Himalaya and its sister contract from 835 to 420 remaining
  days, making them genuinely half-complete under India's national-yard rules.
- Stages the first super-heavy contract from 1939 at 140 IC and the sister ship
  from 1941 at 180 IC.
- Uses unique event-reserved ship identities so scripted fleets no longer
  duplicate ships the player already built from the normal name pool.

## Aviation Research

- Reorganizes six overlapping air teams into distinct design, production,
  experimental, frontline doctrine, carrier doctrine and air-staff roles.
- Keeps carrier aviation research available from the opening independence
  event.
- Introduces Tata Air Lines Engineering Works, Walchand's Hindustan Aircraft,
  V. M. Ghatage's Aeronautical Laboratory, No. 1 Squadron, Aspy Engineer's
  Fleet Air Arm and Subroto Mukerjee's Air Staff.
- Normalizes every Indian technology-team CSV row to the engine's 39-field
  schema.

## Compatibility

- The Tokyo repair is designed for the current November 1941 autosave and does
  not edit the save file.
- Force, fleet and technology-team redesigns apply fully to new campaigns.
