# A Union Before Midnight 4.2.0-alpha.4

> Withdrawn: the ministry-register migration and triennial cabinet experiment
> were incompatible with established campaign minister records. Alpha 5
> restores the complete Alpha 3 government system while retaining the other
> Alpha 4 improvements.

Alpha 4 is a save-compatible campaign systems pass based on play through the
early 1940s. It targets the recurring usability problems that remained after
the content expansion without resetting the world state or granting free
modernization.

## Manpower and formations

- Annual trained reserve classes now begin at 60 manpower before policy,
  service-law and wartime additions. A territorial army under universal
  national service receives 115 manpower per peacetime class.
- Existing campaigns reconcile the 25-manpower difference for every annual
  class already raised. The current 1934-1939 ledger therefore receives 150,
  while untouched future classes receive nothing early.
- Immediate event formations use the latest researched model, enter named
  corps at field-ready strength and deduct manpower explicitly.
- Event production contracts retain normal daily IC and manpower costs but
  start the first item with meaningful funded progress. Extending the serial
  line does not reproduce the progress award.
- Gurkha formations now deploy into a named Delhi corps instead of silently
  entering the force pool.

## Himalaya and government

- Nepalese and Bhutanese refusals now lead to three distinct responses:
  respect sovereignty, apply frontier pressure, or force a settlement at the
  cost of dissent, belligerence and war.
- The colonial duplicate minister pool has been removed. Four complete,
  historically grounded constitutional slates remain.
- A one-time save migration preserves the incumbent mandate and retires the
  obsolete minister records.
- A national mandate recurs every three years from 1939 to 1963, allowing the
  player to appoint a complete Gandhi-Nehru, Rau-Visvesvaraya or Bose cabinet.
  Ordinary domestic-policy movement no longer chooses the government by
  accident.

## Upgrades and controls

- Paid cavalry-to-motorized, militia-to-infantry and garrison-to-infantry
  conversions are enabled at a useful discount from new construction.
- Paid battleship/battlecruiser-to-carrier, cruiser-to-light/escort-carrier and
  transport-to-light/escort-carrier refits are faster and cheaper.
- A zero upgrade slider still means zero progress. The Blood and Iron passive
  free-upgrade behavior remains disabled.
- The Steam Deck profile adds Shift multi-selection, Ctrl-number assignment,
  ten control-group slots, quick save, map/statistics shortcuts and an explicit
  keyboard control while retaining mouse-precise trackpad input.

## Compatibility

- Existing V4.2 saves are supported. The reserve and ministry migrations fire
  once after the campaign advances by up to two days.
- A new campaign is recommended only when evaluating the complete 1933 balance
  curve; it is not required to continue an established V4.2 campaign.
- V3 and pre-V4 saves remain unsupported.
