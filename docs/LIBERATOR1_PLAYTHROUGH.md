# LIBERATOR1 - the sovereign-liberator playthrough

**Historical first-revision guide.** LIBERATOR2 supersedes its Soviet threshold,
Chinese options, finale eligibility and activation names. Use
[the current LIBERATOR2 guide](LIBERATOR2_LONG_WAR_OUTCOMES.md) for this playthrough.
The original liberation petition, treaty, Suez and manpower mechanics below remain.

Additive gameplay pack, 7 September 2026. Base remains Alpha 27 with the local
HYBRID-WORLD1 visuals and MENU-SAFETY1 exits. This is **not Alpha 28**.

## Resume and enable

After installation, fully exit and restart Darkest Hour. Select **AUBM Terrain
Prototype P1**, then continue **autosave, 1 August 1940**. No new campaign or
rebuilt save is required. Both changed event files are already referenced by
that save; its gameplay state is not edited.

In Decisions, select **LIBERATOR1 - India's Liberation Programme**, then
**Enable the sovereign-liberator campaign**. It costs nothing, declares no war,
changes no alliance and repays no historical reward. The named decision is the
in-game pack identifier; the existing graphical loading badge is unchanged.
The **LIBERATOR1 - Liberation Campaign Guide** remains available afterwards.
All new decisions and guide pages have an effect-free Cancel.

The programme is for sovereign India from 1940 onward. Joining a major coalition
or accepting another route's commitment suspends its live eligibility; completed
credit is retained, never reset for another payout. It does not remove the normal
route lock or let India join conflicting alliances.

## Recommended order

1. Stay neutral while preparing supplies and deployments. Japan attacking Britain
   or America does not automatically make India a belligerent.
2. Intervene deliberately when ready, or respond if Japan attacks India. Choose
   **Act as the World Balancer** when the Sovereign Indian War Charter appears.
3. Earn the existing Southeast Asian liberation milestones. Then negotiate actual
   independence and reconstruction partnerships using the new decisions.
4. Complete the existing operational theatre: two distinct land results and one
   sea lane, or one land result and two sea lanes. The existing fleet and base
   requirements still apply; this pack does not manufacture naval victory.
5. Optionally force the Nanjing government to choose between Tokyo and sovereignty.
6. Seek the existing southern armistice with Japan when eligible. Two live sovereign
   partners plus the operational achievement and peace with Japan earn the new
   southern dividend and unlock a liberation-based World Balancer culmination.
7. Consider a Soviet war later, ideally when Soviet forces are occupied elsewhere.
   Win the southern Soviet milestone, negotiate sovereign republics, then fund
   their reconstruction. Fighting Japan and the USSR simultaneously is optional.
8. End all Indian wars for **Delhi Conference: Two Wars, No Empire**. Suez is an
   optional side achievement, never a compulsory third war or a conference gate.

## Liberation becomes a political outcome

The existing **Indian Command Liberates...** milestones still require previous
Japanese occupation, restored hubs and a real Indian contribution. The new
programme does not award these for neutral occupation or for defending territory
which Japan never captured.

After the milestone, an independence petition becomes available when the legal
owner has the complete release territory, controlled by that owner or India:

| Settlement | Owner that can accept | Required release territory, including campaign hubs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Malaya / MLY | Britain | Singapore, Kuala Lumpur, Kota Bahru, Alor Setar, Ipoh, Kuantan, Johor Bahru |
| Indonesia / INO | Dutch East Indies or Netherlands | Batavia, Tjilegon, Bandoeng, Tjirebon, Soerabaja, Jogjakarta, Malang, Madura |
| Vietnam / VIE | French Indochina or France | Hanoi, Dong Hoi, Da Nang, Saigon, Can Tho |
| Philippines / PHI | United States | All 16 minimum Philippine provinces if creating an absent state; restored Manila and Davao if ending existing US puppet status |

The petition costs **100 money**, with **65% acceptance / 35% refusal**. A refusal
allows reconsideration after 180 days. A changed front or vanished recipient
invalidates the proposal without transferring land; the dispatched mission's
cost is not refunded. A release uses the game's normal minimum/extra territory
rules: additional legally owned provinces can transfer too. No Indian divisions
change allegiance. There is no blanket annexation or seizure of friendly land.

An already independent qualifying state skips the release petition. Offer its
**Equal Reconstruction Treaty** for **200 money and 500 supplies**:

- **75% acceptance:** aid, Indian military access, goodwill and an Indian security
  guarantee. India earns **+1 TC modifier and -1 dissent**, once per partner.
- **25% refusal:** the state keeps the reconstruction aid but refuses military
  cooperation; India gains no treaty reward. Reconsideration is possible after
  180 days.

These are not Indian puppets and do not enter an Indian alliance. A later loss
of independence or key hubs removes their eligibility for further combined
outcomes, but does not repay or reset a historical reward.

Limitations are explicit: Vietnam is not a complete Laos/Cambodia settlement;
Singapore may be part of the Malayan release; and country-release behaviour must
still be confirmed in the engine. The programme does not split territory between
multiple legal owners without consent.

## China after Nationalist defeat

**Nanjing Must Choose: Tokyo or Chinese Sovereignty** requires India to control
**Nanjing, Shanghai and Wuhan**, while fighting Japan and its allied Nanjing puppet.
Nationalist China must remain absent; Nanjing and Wuhan must still legally belong
to the Nanjing state.

Offer **250 money and 750 supplies** in diplomatic effort:

- **60%:** the Nanjing government ends Japanese puppet status and leaves Tokyo's
  alliance with the documented white-peace withdrawal setting. Its inherited
  alliance wars end; India's Japanese war does not. It grants India access.
- **40%:** refusal, with a new offer possible after 90 days if leverage remains.

Once independence, peace with India and restored Chinese control of Nanjing and
Wuhan are verified, India gains **1,500 supplies, +2 TC modifier and -3 dissent**,
plus an Indian guarantee of Chinese sovereignty. This counts as one partner.

This preserves **Republic of China-Nanjing (U87)**, including its existing units
and state. It does not resurrect Chiang, change the country tag, or claim a
democratic restoration. **Shanghai is Japanese-owned in the August save** and
is not transferred by the Chinese agreement; its fate belongs to the Japanese
war and settlement.

## Defend Suez without owning it

The automatic defence record begins only when:

- India is at war with Germany or Italy, and that actual enemy controls
  El Alamein, Alexandria or Cairo.
- Suez and Port Said are controlled by India, Britain or Egypt, with India at
  peace with Britain and Egypt.
- At least three Indian land divisions are in either Suez or Port Said.

Maintain the position and garrison through a **30-day observation period**.
Daily checks mark an attempt invalid if the garrison, friendly canal control,
relevant war or sovereign route is lost. Reoccupying it on the final day cannot
repair an already invalidated attempt. Another attempt can start after the old
review closes if the threat conditions recur.

Success gives **1,500 supplies, 150 money, +1 TC modifier and -2 dissent**, once.
This recognises sustained defensive deployment; it does not claim to count
individual battles or prevent every possible interruption between daily checks.
It neither transfers the canal nor automatically grants access, joins an alliance
or declares a war. Arrange the required access before deploying.

## Soviet reconstruction and the final payoff

The existing Soviet victory and negotiation rules remain unchanged:
**Baku plus either Tashkent or Astrakhan** opens the limited Soviet result.
Releasing sovereign Central Asian republics additionally requires their complete
qualifying territory and Moscow's acceptance.

After peace with Moscow and an actual sovereign settlement, **Reconstruction
Beyond the Soviet Frontier** offers a once-only programme:

- Pay **300 money and 1,000 supplies**.
- Gain **+1 research modifier, +2 supply-output modifier and -2 dissent**.
- At least one qualifying Central Asian republic must remain independent,
  friendly and outside Japan's alliance. A base-rights armistice is insufficient.
- The Japanese war may continue; this is not gated behind global peace.

**The Southern Peace Has Independent Signatories** separately recognises peace
with Japan, the Southeast Asian operational achievement, and two live partners
from Malaya, Indonesia, Vietnam, Philippines or the Chinese settlement. It pays
**750 supplies, 250 money and -2 dissent** once and supplies an alternative
completion condition for the existing World Balancer arc. It does not award a
second primary charter or fabricate the old national-theatre flags.

Finally, **Delhi Conference: Two Wars, No Empire** requires both programmes,
surviving qualifying partners, and **no remaining Indian war**. Choose one:

- **Open community:** +2 research modifier, +3 supply-output modifier, -3 dissent.
- **Defensive logistics network:** +3 TC modifier, 2,500 supplies, -1 dissent.

This extra two-front legacy is deliberately separate from, and does not reset,
the normal once-only charter congress. No country is released by this final
conference; its members must already exist.

## Manpower and balance

**Wartime Replacement Training: LIBERATOR1** adds one paid class per calendar
year, 1940-1964, while sovereign India fights Japan or the USSR, fields at least
100 land divisions and has fewer than 400 manpower.

Pay **900 supplies, 100 money and +1 dissent** for **180 manpower**. There is
one allowance per year, not per enemy. Existing mobilisation, emergency levies
and annual reserve classes are untouched. Under the reviewed policies, recurring
event capacity becomes 270 normal wartime reserves + 180 new training, or up to
700 with the eligible 250-manpower emergency levy. Thresholds and costs still
apply. This does not guarantee 1,000 total manpower annually; ordinary recruitment
and casualties still matter.

## Installation and verification boundary

The scoped installer backs up the original two modules and the current autosave
pair, confirms the save already references both modules, and hash-checks protected
saves, scenario files, visuals, launcher settings and the other installation.
It refuses to install while Darkest Hour is running. The installed receipt is
`LIBERATOR1.json`; backups are under this repository's ignored `tmp` directory.

Tests execute generated predicates against synthetic campaign states, covering
wrong ownership, stale callbacks, refused terms, missing recipients, route changes,
reward duplication, two-front peace, daily Suez interruption and annual manpower
limits. Existing wartime, route, Southeast Asian, diplomatic and menu audits are
also retained. Script tests are **not an engine playtest**. Human acceptance still
needs to confirm decision presentation, normal release transfers, the Chinese
alliance exit and delayed event delivery. No game is launched by these tools.
