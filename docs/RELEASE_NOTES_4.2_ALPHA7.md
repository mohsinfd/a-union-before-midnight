# A Union Before Midnight 4.2.0-alpha.7

## Strategic Consolidation

- Moved the inherited Raj technical archive from 1937 to the independence
  settlement. It now runs only when a genuine baseline gap exists.
- Added an India-only modernization contract matching the proven playtest
  timetable, then improves its IC burden: all paid land, air and naval
  upgrades use 25% cost and 10% time. Zero upgrade IC still means zero
  progress, and other countries retain stock rules.
- Added a 250-manpower national register and raised annual trained intake to
  150 before army, civic-service, wartime-service and active-war bonuses.
- Rebalanced emergency call-ups and national-service laws around equipment,
  officers and industrial capacity rather than an implausible population
  shortage.
- Added the 1937 federal income-tax settlement and 1940 war-finance board for
  sustainable research and diplomatic funding.
- Limited automatic constitutional repair to the Head of State and Head of
  Government. Working ministers remain replaceable by the player.
- Replaced six scattered war-entry decisions with one persistent Emergency
  War Cabinet. India may join Britain, Germany, Japan or the Soviet Union;
  fight Japan, Germany or the USSR independently; or defer without losing the
  choice.
- Rebuilt the Goa refusal as an escalation ladder covering pressure, civil
  resistance, renewed ultimata, British mediation, blockade and war. Existing
  saves that already own Goa receive the missing integration settlement.
- Added state-based conclusions for Indian intervention in Abyssinia and
  Spain, so foreign event-chain divergence cannot erase the consequences.
- Replaced technical placeholder research-team names with historical people,
  firms and institutions. The carrier-design, naval-air and fleet-doctrine
  group is available from independence and has complete aviation specialties.
- Added distinct historical minister traits for Bhaskar Soman, Thimayya,
  Arjan Singh, Aspy Engineer, Mehar Singh, Jyoti Basu and Lal Bahadur Shastri,
  while preserving the existing cabinet structure and portraits.

## Verification

- Full direct-Darkest-Hour rebase is repeatable across 3,008 overlay files.
- Static validation: 0 errors and 0 warnings.
- Inherited research parity: 232 India technologies against 232 British
  baseline technologies, with 0 missing.
- Opening reserve after standard commitments: 730 manpower; 700 after the
  optional full airfield-security programme.
- 1934-39 trained intake: 1,200 on a lean path and 1,500 on a typical path.
- All event procurement remains single-unit, current-model and normal-cost.
- Construction, combat pacing, art provenance and stable-rendering gates pass.

## Campaign Compatibility

Alpha 7 is designed for a new 1933 campaign. The modernization, Goa repair and
finance guarantees can assist existing V4 saves, but only a fresh start gives
the intended inheritance, manpower, leadership and strategic-decision curve.
