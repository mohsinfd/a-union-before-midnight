# A Union Before Midnight 4.2.0-alpha.1

This release is the prewar agency, research parity and historical command pass.
It requires a new 1933 campaign for scenario technologies and leader records.

## Strategic agency

- Britain, Germany, Japan, China, Siam and the Soviet Union now answer India's
  1937 partnership initiatives through AI-weighted foreign events.
- Foreign governments can accept equal cooperation, impose political terms or
  reject Delhi. India may accept those terms or change direction.
- The independent Asian route can form a real Delhi Pact with China, Siam or
  both. Alliance options explicitly warn that they carry automatic war entry.
- Abyssinia, Spain, China, Anschluss, Munich, Prague and Poland now produce
  recipient-country effects rather than only Indian relations and currencies.
- The 1939 War Cabinet provides an explicit Allied, German, independent-war or
  armed-neutrality choice and reconciles every strategy flag.

## Research, money and manpower

- India begins with the full 232-technology British/Raj 1933 baseline.
- Essential research institutions receive calendar fallbacks so one missed
  policy flag cannot disable an entire service branch.
- A modest permanent revenue stream prevents decisions from consuming every
  rupee while retaining real opportunity cost.
- Annual trained recruit classes and a wartime reserve decision prevent one
  early manpower choice from permanently starving the army.

## Historical command

- Twelve additional real officers from the undivided Indian services join the
  bespoke roster with distinct portraits and plausible accelerated careers.
- K. M. Cariappa, K. S. Thimayya, S. M. Shrinagesh, Ram Dass Katari, Subroto
  Mukerjee and other abbreviated stock records now use full names and revised
  skills.
- The 1936 officer expansion is calendar-guaranteed. Land command capacity is
  80 in 1933, 92 in 1936, 127 in 1939 and 189 in 1942.

## Interface and safety

- Priced decisions remain visible before the treasury can fund them. A compact
  programme ledger shows every option and its minimum money, supplies and
  manpower reserves.
- A decision becomes selectable when its least expensive valid programme is
  affordable. Every action retains its own reserve check, so a visible premium
  programme cannot drive a stockpile below zero.
- Event button text is validated against Darkest Hour's 58-byte UI limit.
- New leader portraits are validated as 36x50 indexed BMPs.
- A cold-start campaign audit checks cadence, research parity, leader capacity,
  unavoidable cash drains, world-state effects and structural choice variety.
- The build remains a direct Darkest Hour Full overlay and does not launch the
  game during validation or deployment.

## Engine boundary

Same-type model upgrades remain automatic. Darkest Hour's cross-type upgrade
target dialog is compiled and per division; the event language has no bulk
target command. Model files can impose one global preset destination, but not
a country-scoped or selected-unit batch choice. Cross-type paths remain
available, while unsafe replacement events and destructive save edits are
excluded.
