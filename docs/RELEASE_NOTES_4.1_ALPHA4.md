# A Union Before Midnight 4.1.0-alpha.4

## Manpower and mobilization

- Added an annual trained-reserve class from 1934 through 1964. Every integrated India receives 35 base manpower; army organization, civic service, wartime service and active war can raise the annual class to 135.
- Made the annual intake state-based and save-compatible. No early policy choice can cancel later recruitment, and an existing campaign receives only its current year's class.
- Rebuilt the 1942 National Service Debate. It no longer requires manpower to select a manpower policy, no longer spends its award on compulsory production queues and no longer depends on a naval-logistics event.
- Added a once-per-calendar-year Emergency Reserve Call-Up when India is at war and below 100 manpower. The three choices trade 70-180 manpower against supplies and dissent.
- Updated service and provincial-army buttons to show both immediate manpower and recurring annual effects.

## Naval procurement

- Restored inherited construction progress for named naval programmes while preserving India's current researched models and the engine's normal IC cost.
- Arabian Sea and Bay of Bengal fleet hulls now arrive in fitting-out with 120-210 days remaining.
- Oceanic carrier, cruiser, escort and submarine hulls begin with 150-540 days remaining according to hull size.
- Split 1941 escort and submarine serials into individually named one-hull lines, preventing a completed first hull from making later extensions artificially cheap.
- Restricted completion progress in validation to single naval hulls in five approved fleet events, with a hard ceiling of 730 remaining days. Fixed event production costs remain forbidden.

## Release gates

- Added regression checks for annual recruitment, low-manpower emergency mobilization, National Service independence and approved naval hull progress.
- Existing V4.1 saves remain compatible; no province, tag, technology or unit-model migration is required.
