# A Union Before Midnight 4.2 Alpha 16

Alpha 16 is a player-information and specialist-availability release built on
the complete Alpha 15 equipment progression.

## Chindit Programme

- Moves the Long-Range Penetration Group from a hidden war-only 1941 gate to a
  visible preparatory programme from 1939 when India is at war or Japan and
  China are at war.
- Adds an unconditional 1941 fallback so a peaceful or ahistorical Asian war
  state cannot permanently hide the formation.
- Retains the Basic Infantry technology, Dehradun control and full money,
  supply and manpower requirements.
- Commissions the existing 77th Indian Penetration Division and its complete
  Chindit equipment ladder; it does not grant a free additional formation.

## Diplomatic Disclosure Standard

- Shows exact accept, counteroffer and refusal odds before every audited major
  foreign-policy commitment.
- Labels proposal-specific rolls as fixed and states that relations, IC and
  force size do not secretly modify them.
- Labels direct alliance entry, war declarations, commands and settlements as
  having no foreign roll when the result is immediate.
- Preserves Tokyo as a genuinely tiered negotiation: the interface lists every
  influence contribution, IC threshold and army, navy or air-force test.
- Shows the Allied settlement's participant, regional, senior and hegemon
  battlefield requirements and the foreign odds attached to each tier.
- Covers the early British, German, Soviet, Japanese, Delhi Pact, Himalayan,
  Ceylon, Goa, Abyssinian, China and Anschluss choices.
- Covers later Allied, German, Soviet, Japanese and non-aligned alliance,
  corridor, intervention and settlement systems.

## Release Safety

- Adds a 374-check diplomatic release gate that reads the real foreign
  `ai_chance` values and fails the build if player-visible odds drift from them.
- Extends specialist validation to require the 1939 Chindit pressure trigger,
  Sino-Japanese War condition and unconditional 1941 fallback.
- Existing saves can use the revised decisions and Chindit availability if the
  relevant events have not already fired or been slept. No save edit is used.
