# R11 country campaign choices

Eight new country-specific decisions plus three earlier Chinese partner offers. Existing settlement/peace machinery is retained. No new alliance route is selected.

| Malaya: rubber, tin and a working economy | 1941 | Pacific war begun; Japan or friendly Malayan treaty government holds Kuala Lumpur | Buy rubber and metal stocks / Buy fuel feedstock and campaign supplies |

Every funded choice has its own selected-branch marker, complete upfront bill, due-date map/research recheck, exact refund and final one-time receipt. Closing schedules no reminder; dismissal is permanent for that offer only.

Unit models and manpower costs are read from the clean Blood and Iron technologies/templates. Unit funding reserves that model's complete manpower. Units are issued as normal equipped formations, with ordinary combat and maintenance; no zero-progress production order or retired custom class is used. Native mobilisation and engine deployment behaviour remain runtime checks.

Friendly neutral treaties qualify only for stated civilian/merchant programmes. No new declaration, alliance, mastery, core, province transfer or peace command is issued. Physical supplies/resources enter the national pool; convoy delivery remains the player's responsibility.

Chinese aid opens from 1938, expedition/home-defence choice and reconstruction from 1939, once the actual puppet exists. Chinese military aid now grants 35 local manpower and 3,000 supplies instead of inserting an unfunded infantry order.

An absent Philippine Commonwealth can now be released from occupied American-owned Philippine territory during a Japanese-American war. Only Japanese-held Philippine provinces transfer; American peace is not issued. Both puppet and protected-neutral outcomes have been checked.

## China: who holds Shanghai?

The landing force is needed farther inland. Choose two ordinary 1931-model Garrison divisions to hold the port, or one ordinary 1936 Infantry division that can follow the advance. Both arrive equipped in Shanghai. Garrisons cannot march; these are ordinary formations, not the retired custom classes.

| Choice | Full upfront bill | Wait |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hold Shanghai with two Garrisons | 160 money, 2000 supplies, 20 manpower | 120 days |
| Raise one mobile Infantry division | 200 money, 2800 supplies, 13 manpower | 180 days |

## China: beyond Wuhan

Wuhan opens the approach to the interior. Fund two ordinary 1935 Mountain Infantry divisions at Wuhan for rough-country fighting, or a casualty evacuation service that returns 30 manpower and 1,500 supplies to Japan once. The evacuation award is a finite recovered reserve, not daily recruitment.

| Choice | Full upfront bill | Wait |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Train two Mountain Infantry divisions | 220 money, 4000 supplies, 26 manpower | 180 days |
| Recover 30 manpower and 1500 supplies | 140 money, 3000 supplies | 120 days |

## Philippines: aircraft for the new front

Manila can support more than a flag on the map. Choose two ordinary Interceptor wings to contest the air, or two Naval Bomber wings to patrol the surrounding seas. They deploy equipped at Manila using the listed prewar models; research, the manpower bill and normal oil/supply use remain necessary. Keep Japanese control until delivery.

| Choice | Full upfront bill | Wait |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deploy two Interceptor wings at Manila | 220 money, 3200 supplies, 1.5 manpower, 500 metal, 500 oil | 150 days |
| Deploy two Naval Bomber wings at Manila | 260 money, 4000 supplies, 4.4 manpower, 600 metal, 800 oil | 180 days |

## Philippines: soldiers or shipping?

After an agreement, decide how the islands fit the next campaign. With a Philippine puppet, fund two Japanese 1936 Infantry divisions at Tokyo for later deployment. Any friendly treaty government can instead support a one-time merchant contract: 160 convoy transports, 40 escorts and 4,000 Japanese supplies. Neutral terms remain neutral; no war is inherited by this programme.

| Choice | Full upfront bill | Wait |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Raise two Infantry divisions at Tokyo | 300 money, 4200 supplies, 26 manpower | 210 days |
| Buy the inter-island merchant package | 200 money, 1800 supplies | 120 days |

## Indonesia: put Soerabaja to work

Use Soerabaja as a funded naval workshop. Commission two ordinary Destroyers or one Light Cruiser, with models no later than 1938, at this port. These are actual ships, not convoy counters or production-queue entries. They require their normal crews, fuel and maintenance. Japan must retain control of Soerabaja until completion.

| Choice | Full upfront bill | Wait |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Commission two Destroyers at Soerabaja | 260 money, 4200 supplies, 2.5 manpower, 1200 metal, 600 oil | 240 days |
| Commission one Light Cruiser at Soerabaja | 300 money, 5000 supplies, 0.75 manpower, 1600 metal, 800 oil | 300 days |

## Indonesia: what leaves the islands?

Batavia and Palembang can support one large export mission. Choose a 12,000-oil cargo, or 2,000 rare materials, 4,000 metal and 60 convoy transports. Deliveries enter Japan's national reserve, not a local depot. The oil choice requires Japanese oil below 12,000 at funding and receipt; check storage before ordering. Friendly puppet or neutral treaty control is accepted. This creates no continuing resource bonus.

| Choice | Full upfront bill | Wait |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Order the 12000-oil cargo | 260 money, 2800 supplies, 4000 energy | 150 days |
| Order minerals and merchant hulls | 260 money, 2800 supplies | 150 days |

## Malaya: Singapore after the fighting

Singapore offers a choice between a spare-parts service and merchant replacements. The service adds 20 percentage points to Japan's reinforcement efficiency for 360 days, across land, air and naval forces; fund the reinforcement slider to use it. It restores no sunk ships. The other package supplies 200 convoy transports, 60 escorts and 4,000 supplies once. Japan must retain control of Singapore until the work finishes.

| Choice | Full upfront bill | Wait |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fund a one-year spare-parts service | 180 money, 3000 supplies, 1500 metal, 1000 oil | 90 days |
| Replace merchant ships and operating stocks | 220 money, 2400 supplies, 1000 metal | 150 days |

## Malaya: rubber, tin and a working economy

Holding Kuala Lumpur, or trading with a friendly Malayan treaty government, opens one funded export programme. Choose 2,000 rare materials and 4,000 metal for factories and shipbuilding, or 16,000 energy and 6,000 supplies for fuel conversion and a long campaign. These are finite Japanese stock deliveries. No occupation, puppet, neutrality or base term is changed by either choice.

| Choice | Full upfront bill | Wait |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Buy rubber and metal stocks | 220 money, 2200 supplies | 150 days |
| Buy fuel feedstock and campaign supplies | 220 money, 2200 supplies | 150 days |
