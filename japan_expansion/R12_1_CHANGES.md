# Japan Rising R12.1 — activation and island shipping

The six R12 specialist introductions now request a single guarded transaction. That transaction activates the type, applies researched effects and models, creates the free equipment, restores the new formation's strength, and only then records completion. Its eligibility remains valid if the human follow-up is acknowledged after the first event has finished. Requests cannot reopen while pending. Each command also checks eligibility at execution, preventing a previously opened completion from duplicating units or bonuses. Losing all usable home bases while waiting returns the request to its initial waiting state without consuming the gift.

All six existing introduction IDs, upgrade paths and original artwork are retained. No additional specialist classes are installed in this maintenance revision. A new 1933 R12.1 game is the intended entry point; no campaign save was edited or migrated.

## Island supply shipping

All 31 dormant local garrison templates remain, with their existing locations, permanent 25-strength caps and call-up costs. The first successful main-island, additional-base or Haikou call-up also releases **180 convoy transports and 90 convoy escorts**, once across all three groups. This is a finite national shipping allowance. It does not add map transport flotillas, combat destroyers or automatic escort missions.

The paid prewar merchant programme retains its **120-money, 600-supply, 150-day** bill. Its choices now yield **160 transports/40 escorts** or **100 transports/80 escorts**, replacing 120/25 and 70/60. It remains optional and available once.

The revision adds capacity. Automatic route creation, depot formation, oil selection and escort assignment remain engine convoy functions; they have not been observed in a running game.

## Existing naval content

- From 1935: carrier/CVL/CAG production or destroyer/light-cruiser/submarine production investment.
- 1934 carrier safety commission: aircrew training or protected decks, plus the Tomozuru and 1935 typhoon structural choices.
- From 1938: additional repair ports at Kobe/Yokohama.
- After the required southern campaign conditions: Soerabaja can commission two researched Destroyers or one Light Cruiser; this is paid, takes time and requires a working port.
- Merchant orders, southern port agreements and regional shipping contracts add convoy transports/escorts. These are not warships on the map.
- From 1942 and required research: the R12 Akizuki first issue provides an AA escort flotilla.
