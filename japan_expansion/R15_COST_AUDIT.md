The carrier-recovery branch was enlarged after this comparison exposed an excessively favourable shore-air alternative. The paid fleet now contains two Light Carriers, two Light CAGs and four Destroyers, takes330days and ties up the major commitment. Shore aviation remains quicker and cheaper, and depends on usable airfields. The fleet buys mobile sea coverage and escorted hulls rather than matching bomber firepower on land.

Packets are finite. Stock purchases do not consume a normal production slot after payment; the extra commitment delay, crew requirements, location and alternative forgone are part of the price. These calculations support comparisons; they do not prove equal combat utility.
