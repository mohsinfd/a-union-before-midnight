These are candidates for a subsequent content revision. No additional type registry, technology hook, free issue or artwork is installed for them in R12.1. The objective is operational reach, sustained replacement or protection that merits actual production, with normal full-strength equipment and research-year upgrade paths.

The Smithsonian's Zero history specifically describes the requirement to escort bombers to distant Chinese targets: https://airandspace.si.edu/stories/editorial/mitsubishi-a6m-zero-fighter . This supports the long-range fighter role; the proposed game modifier is a design choice rather than a quoted aircraft specification.

The proposed percentages are design targets, not historical performance measurements or installed modifiers. The first two would be valuable before or around Pearl Harbor, ocean submarines would extend the naval campaign, and economical escorts would support a longer war. Dates must prevent advanced postwar equipment appearing early. Each needs real parent-effect, minister, conversion, upgrade, deployment and asset checks before release; the R12 first-issue sequencing pattern must not be repeated.
