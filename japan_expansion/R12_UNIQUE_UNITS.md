# Japan Rising R12.1 — Japanese formations and equipment

Start a new 1933 campaign in **Japan Rising JX1 Alpha2** and confirm **R12.1** in the scenario/welcome. The new unit and attachment registries require the new campaign. Saved campaigns are not edited. The game has not been launched during development or the cold audit.

## Free introductions

There is no purchase decision for the first issue. After the date and research below, an automatic introduction unlocks production and provides the listed equipment. It charges **no money, manpower or supplies**. The three formations arrive at full strength at an operational Japanese home base; attachments enter the deployment pool for you to assign. Maintain their ordinary supplies/fuel and allow deployed formations time to recover organisation.

| First available | Required research | First issue | What makes it useful |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1935 | Improved Destroyer | **2 Type 93 Oxygen Torpedo attachments** | Initial fitting adds 3 surface attack and 2 convoy attack, at 0.55 supplies/day. Fits eligible Destroyers, Akizuki escorts, Light Cruisers and Heavy Cruisers. |
| 1937 | 1936 Infantry Division | **2 Dokuritsu Konsei Mixed Brigades** | Initial attachment adds 8 suppression, 8 defence and 3 soft attack. Occupation and defended positions; 4-manpower formation and speed cap 4 make it a deliberate alternative to Military Police. |
| 1938 | Naval Bomber 1938 | **1 Maritime Flying Boat Wing** | H6K first model: 2,125 km range versus 1,250, and surface detection 3 versus 1. Naval attack 3 versus 4; higher construction cost/time. |
| 1942 | Modern Destroyer | **1 Akizuki Air-Defence Flotilla** | Initial AA attack 9 versus 6; surface attack 5.6 versus 7. Full 1.5-manpower crew, base IC cost +15% and time +5%. |
| 1942 | 1941 Airborne Infantry Division | **1 Teishin Airborne Division** | Full native Paratrooper firepower and 9 manpower, organisation 43 versus 35, morale 48 versus 40, supply use -10%. Cost +15%, time +10%; needs normal transport aircraft and airborne mission conditions. |
| 1942 | Improved Light Tank | **2 Ka-Mi Naval Tank Detachments** | Initial attachment: soft attack +4, defence +2, shore attack +15 and river attack +12 percentage points. Fuel demand 1/day versus the inherited amphibious armour brigade's 3/day. Smaller anti-armour punch and armour coverage; consumes an attachment slot. |

Dates are the earliest service dates, not promises that the required research will finish automatically. A late unlock catches up to the latest model whose year and research requirements are both met. If the home ports/airfields are unavailable, the formation issue waits; it is not lost or repeatedly charged. There is no quarterly claim/reminder chain.

Further copies use normal production. Starting first-model prices before technology, ministries and production programmes are: Type 93 0.65 IC/100 days; Mixed Brigade 3 IC/120 days; Ka-Mi 4.5 IC/140 days; Akizuki 1.495 IC/598 days; H6K 17.25 IC/264 days; Teishin 11.5 IC/451 days. The free initial issues represent a limited funded service introduction, not an unlimited replacement source.

Older model checks cannot roll back a newer researched model. Ordinary researched upgrades still require normal upgrade expenditure.

## Production, doctrine and ministers

The specialist divisions inherit their parent terrain/weather role and normal attachments. Japanese concrete Destroyer/Naval Bomber/Paratrooper effects are mirrored, including penalties. Existing preparation programmes apply whether completed before or after the new class becomes available. Research has separate one-time records so unlocking the class cannot apply a doctrine twice.

Type 93 competes with radar, AA and ASW fittings for an eligible ship's attachment capacity. Mixed Brigades fit Infantry, Garrisons and Militia. Naval Tank Detachments fit Infantry and Marines. They do not occupy an extra division command or amphibious-assault slot. The old Base Force, Jungle Light and SNLF division classes stay retired.

These are game-scale representations of historical equipment and formations. Flying boats use normal airfields and maritime missions; they have no invented independent anti-submarine damage system. Teishin is represented at full game-division scale so a small historical detachment does not consume a full command slot while fighting as a fraction of a division.
