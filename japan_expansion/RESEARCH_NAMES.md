# Added Japanese research teams — R10

The clean Blood and Iron Japanese file contains 54 entries. This mod adds ten entries, for 64 total across all dates, not 64 teams available in 1933. R10 replaces all ten generic English labels with actual Japanese names in ASCII romanisation for the game's font. IDs, portraits and specialties are retained. Research ratings and specialties are gameplay assignments, not historical qualifications or claims of ten new independent research establishments.

The escort command no longer appears in 1941, before its actual establishment in November 1943. The munitions ministry replaces the invented reconstruction institute and opens in its actual establishment year, 1943. Year-only game availability cannot represent the exact month. Other availability dates represent access to that research profile, not founding dates. Continuation beyond historical dissolution in 1945 represents the mod's alternate-history campaign.

The infantry school retains the mountain-training specialty as an army training abstraction; this is not a claim that a dedicated historical Army Mountain Warfare School existed. The Shanghai formation represents practical marine expertise, not a fictional training centre.
